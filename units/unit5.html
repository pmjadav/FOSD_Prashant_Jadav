Unit 5

Memory Management

Core concepts, analysis points and suggested learning activities.

Topics Covered

  • Relocation, protection and sharing
  • Logical and physical organization
  • Fixed and dynamic partitioning
  • Internal/external fragmentation and compaction
  • First fit, best fit, worst fit and next fit
  • Paging and page tables
  • Segmentation
  • TLB and effective access time

Learning Focus

Students should be able to explain mechanisms, analyze examples, solve relevant numerical problems where applicable, and justify design choices using appropriate OS performance and reliability criteria.

Suggested Activities

  • Read the corresponding lecture notes.
  • Work through worked examples and numerical problems.
  • Attempt higher-order questions without referring to the solution.
  • Complete the related practical activity where available.
