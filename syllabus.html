Course & Syllabus

Fundamentals of Operating System Design · CEUC301

Course focus: Understand, analyze and evaluate operating-system mechanisms using performance, reliability, security, scalability and workload characteristics.

Course Outcomes

CO1

Analyze OS design goals and justify architectural choices.

CO2

Analyze processes, threads and scheduling using performance metrics.

CO3

Design and evaluate synchronization and concurrency mechanisms.

CO4

Analyze deadlocks and design prevention, avoidance, detection and recovery strategies.

CO5

Assess memory and virtual-memory designs using fragmentation, latency, security and scalability.

CO6

Evaluate file-system and I/O subsystem designs.

CO7

Synthesize OS design principles across real-time, embedded, mobile and specialized domains.
