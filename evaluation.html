Evaluation Overview

Maximum Marks represent the marks obtained in the respective assessment, while Effective Marks represent the marks contributed to the final course evaluation after applying the prescribed conversion.

What are Effective Marks?

Maximum Marks indicate the marks assigned to an assessment. Effective Marks indicate the marks that the assessment contributes to the final course evaluation.

CIE-1: Maximum Marks = 20; Effective Marks = 6.
