Operating System Projects

Explore OS concepts through practical, self-directed projects.

These projects are suggested for students interested in exploring Operating System concepts through practical implementation. Participation is completely voluntary. Students can select a topic based on their interests and develop a working application, simulator, or visualization to strengthen their understanding of Operating Systems.

Optional Learning Activity

Projects are voluntary learning activities. They are not compulsory and do not contribute to course evaluation or academic marks.

A few helpful suggestions

Development Guidelines

Use whichever of these ideas is helpful for your project.

  • Understand the relevant Operating System concepts before implementation.
  • Start with a small working prototype.
  • Use meaningful input and output examples.
  • Include visualizations wherever they improve understanding.
  • Test the implementation with different scenarios.
  • Explain the algorithms and design decisions in the documentation.
  • Students may explore their own project ideas related to Operating Systems.
